Iot-enabled smart irrigation system using soil moisture sensing🌱
An IoT-enabled smart irrigation system uses soil moisture sensors to continuously monitor the water content in the soil. The sensor data is sent to a microcontroller or cloud platform, where it is analyzed to determine the irrigation requirement. When the soil moisture level falls below a predefined threshold, the system automatically activates the water pump, and it turns off once sufficient moisture is detected. This approach reduces water wastage, improves crop productivity, and enables remote monitoring through mobile or web applications. The system is especially useful for efficient water management in agriculture.
